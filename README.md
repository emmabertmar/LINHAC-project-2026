# What separates power play sequences that lead to goals from those that don't?

Finalists in the LINHAC student competition.

A sports analytics project done at Linköping University, based on Sportlogiq event
data from the 2024-25 season.

The idea was to split every power play into sequences (a gap of more than 10 seconds
starts a new one) and then look at what the successful ones actually do differently.
We looked at zone entry types, puck movement before shots, shot locations, when during
the power play goals happen, and we clustered teams by playing style to see if style is
linked to conversion rate.

## Files

- `powerplay_analysis.ipynb` - all the analysis and plots
- `report.pdf` - the written report, read this one for the actual results and discussion
- `requirements.txt` - pandas, matplotlib, seaborn
